Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Ray Reveals Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the Ray Reveals creator to publish the channel's own videos. This policy explains what data it uses.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and schedules on the Ray Reveals channel.
  • Google Drive: access only to files and folders the app creates, used to queue videos for scheduled posting.
  • Instagram and Facebook: permission to publish Reels to the Ray Reveals accounts.

How data is stored

Login tokens are stored on the creator's own computer in the operating system's credential store, and in the creator's own Google Drive. The app has no server and does not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to upload and schedule the creator's own videos.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Instagram and Facebook settings under Apps and websites.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Ray Reveals YouTube channel.
